# Clitic Doubling 03: Psych-Verbs & Inherent Datives (Costar)

> **Grammar Rule**: With verbs of psychological effect (gustar, costar, molestar, encantar), the dative clitic (*les*) is mandatory even when the full noun phrase is present.

### Context
English: "The engineers find it hard to adapt to the syntax of the new functional language."

### Exercise
(A los ingenieros les cuesta / A los ingenieros cuesta) ___ adaptarse a la sintaxis del nuevo lenguaje funcional.

<!-- SOLUTION
A los ingenieros les cuesta
-->
