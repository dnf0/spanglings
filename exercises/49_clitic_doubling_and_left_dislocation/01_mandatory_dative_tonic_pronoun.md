# Clitic Doubling 01: Mandatory Dative with Tonic Pronouns (A mí me)
<!-- id: b1_clitic_tonic_dative_a_mi | level: B1 | topic: clitic_doubling_and_left_dislocation | type: cloze | concepts: ["clitic_doubling_mandatory_dative"] | prerequisites: ["direct_indirect_pronouns", "gustar_dative_structures"] | grammar_focus: "Mandatory clitic pronoun presence when preceded by a tonic prepositional pronoun." | contrast_note: "Mandatory doubling (*A mí me parece*) vs ungrammatical omission (*A mí parece*)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Whenever a tonic personal pronoun is used after a preposition (*a mí, a ti, a él/ella*), the corresponding unstressed clitic pronoun (*me, te, le*) is strictly mandatory.

### Context
English: "To me it seems that the migration to microservices will solve the latency bottlenecks."

### Exercise
(A mí me parece / A mí parece) ___ que la migración a microservicios resolverá los cuellos de botella de latencia.

<!-- SOLUTION
A mí me parece
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
a mí me parece
-->
