# Executive Leadership 06: Board Resolutions
<!-- id: c1_exec_board_resolution | level: C1 | topic: executive_leadership | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar & Lexicon**: "To approve unanimously" in parliamentary and corporate board protocol is **aprobar por unanimidad**.

### Context
English: "The board of directors resolved to unanimously approve the corporate restructuring proposal."

### Exercise
El consejo directivo acordó (aprobar por unanimidad) ___ la propuesta de reestructuración corporativa.

<!-- SOLUTION
aprobar por unanimidad
-->
