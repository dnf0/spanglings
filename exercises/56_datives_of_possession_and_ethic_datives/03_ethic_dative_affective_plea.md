> **Grammar Rule**: The ethic dative (*dativo ético/afectivo*) adds emotional stake, affection, or personal concern of the speaker (*¡No me llores!*).

### Context
English: "Calm down, everything has a technical solution; don't cry on me and let's find an alternative!"
