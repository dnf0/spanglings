# Inalienable Possession 02: Personal Belongings
<!-- id: b1_dative_inalienable_property | level: B1 | topic: datives_of_possession_and_ethic_datives | type: cloze | concepts: ["dative_of_inalienable_possession", "accidental_se_structures"] | prerequisites: ["direct_indirect_pronouns"] | grammar_focus: "Accidental damage to personal property combines accidental 'se' with the owner's dative clitic and definite article." | contrast_note: "'Se le rompió la pantalla' (dative + article) vs '*se rompió su pantalla'." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Accidental damage or loss of personal property is expressed with accidental *se* + dative clitic (*se le rompió la pantalla*).

### Context
English: "When tripping in the hallway, Carlos had the corporate tablet screen break on him."

### Exercise
Al tropezar en el pasillo, a Carlos (se le rompió / se rompió su) ___ la pantalla de la tableta corporativa.

<!-- SOLUTION
se le rompió
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Se le rompió
-->
