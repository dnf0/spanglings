# Sympathetic Dative 05: Collective Misfortune (Se Nos)
<!-- id: b2_dative_sympathetic_se_nos | level: B2 | topic: datives_of_possession_and_ethic_datives | type: cloze | concepts: ["ethic_affective_sympathetic_dative"] | prerequisites: ["dative_of_inalienable_possession"] | grammar_focus: "'Se nos' conveys shared emotional or operational disruption when an involuntary failure occurs." | contrast_note: "'Se nos cayó el clúster' (shared sympathetic impact) vs 'el clúster se cayó' (detached technical fact)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: *Se nos + verbo* expresses accidental misfortune that collectively affects the speaker's group or organization (*Se nos cayó la base de datos*).

### Context
English: "Just minutes before the customer demo, the entire Kubernetes cluster went down on us."

### Exercise
A escasos minutos de la demostración para clientes, (se nos cayó / se cayó a nosotros) ___ todo el clúster de Kubernetes.

<!-- SOLUTION
se nos cayó
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Se nos cayó
-->
