# Idiom: Consultar con la almohada
<!-- id: c1_idiom_consultar_almohada | level: C1 | topic: idioms | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The idiom 'consultar con la almohada' literally means 'to consult with the pillow' and is used to mean 'to sleep on a decision'.

### Context
English: "I need to sleep on it tonight before deciding."

### Exercise
Necesito consultarlo con la (pillow) ___ esta noche.

<!-- SOLUTION
almohada
