# Idiom: Costar un ojo de la cara
<!-- id: c1_idiom_costar_ojo | level: C1 | topic: idioms | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The idiom 'costar un ojo de la cara' literally means 'to cost an eye from the face' and is equivalent to 'costing an arm and a leg'.

### Context
English: "The car cost me an arm and a leg."

### Exercise
El coche me costó un (eye) ___ de la cara.

<!-- SOLUTION
ojo
-->
