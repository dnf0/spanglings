# Idiom: Dar la lata
<!-- id: c1_idiom_dar_lata | level: C1 | topic: idioms | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The idiom 'dar la lata' literally means 'to give the can' and is used to mean 'to annoy, pester, or be a nuisance'.

### Context
English: "Stop annoying me!"

### Exercise
Deja de darme la (can) ___ .

<!-- SOLUTION
lata
