# Idiom: Tomar el pelo
<!-- id: c1_idiom_tomar_pelo | level: C1 | topic: idioms | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The idiom 'tomar el pelo' literally means 'to take the hair' and is used to mean 'to pull someone's leg / tease'.

### Context
English: "Are you pulling my leg?"

### Exercise
¿Me estás tomando el (hair) ___ ?

<!-- SOLUTION
pelo
