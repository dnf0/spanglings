# Idiom: Meter la pata
<!-- id: c1_idiom_meter_pata | level: C1 | topic: idioms | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The idiom 'meter la pata' literally means 'to put the paw/leg in' and is used to mean 'to make a blunder / put your foot in it'.

### Context
English: "I made a blunder at the meeting yesterday."

### Exercise
Metí la (paw) ___ en la reunión ayer.

<!-- SOLUTION
pata
-->
