# Passive Refleja: Plural Object
<!-- id: c1_passive_refleja_alquilan | level: C1 | topic: passive_refleja | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In passive 'se' (pasiva refleja) with inanimate objects, the verb must agree in number with the plural noun.

### Context
English: "Houses for rent (houses are rented)."

### Exercise
Se (alquilar) ___ casas.

<!-- SOLUTION
alquilan
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
rentan
-->

<!-- DIAGNOSTIC_RULES
