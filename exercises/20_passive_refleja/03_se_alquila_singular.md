# Passive Refleja 03: Singular Subject Agreement
<!-- id: c1_pasiva_refleja_singular | level: C1 | topic: passive_refleja | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: With a singular patient subject, the verb is 3rd person singular.

### Context
English: "Room for rent in the center of Madrid."

### Exercise
Se (alquilar) ___ habitación luminosa en el centro de Madrid.

<!-- SOLUTION
alquila
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
alquila habitación
-->

<!-- DIAGNOSTIC_RULES
pattern: "alquilan" | code: "E0001" | message: "'habitación' is singular -> verb is singular 'alquila'."
-->

<!-- HINTS

Tier 2: 'alquila'.
