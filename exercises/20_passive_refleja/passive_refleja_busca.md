# Passive Refleja vs Impersonal with Human Objects
<!-- id: c1_passive_refleja_busca | level: C1 | topic: passive_refleja | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When 'se' is used with human direct objects introduced by the personal preposition 'a', the construction is impersonal and the verb must remain singular.

### Context
English: "They are looking for the culprits (culprits are searched for)."

### Exercise
Se (buscar) ___ a los culpables.

<!-- SOLUTION
busca
