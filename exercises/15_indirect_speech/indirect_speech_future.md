# Indirect Speech: Future to Conditional
<!-- id: c1_indirect_speech_future | level: C1 | topic: indirect_speech | type: transformation -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In indirect speech, if the reporting verb is in the past, a future tense verb in the direct quote shifts to the conditional tense.

### Context
English: "Direct: 'I will buy the house.' -> Indirect: 'She said she would buy the house.'"

### Exercise
Directo: «Compraré la casa». -> Indirecto: Ella dijo que (comprar) ___ la casa.

<!-- SOLUTION
compraría
