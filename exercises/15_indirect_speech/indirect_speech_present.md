# Indirect Speech: Present to Imperfect
<!-- id: c1_indirect_speech_present | level: C1 | topic: indirect_speech | type: transformation -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In indirect speech, if the reporting verb is in the past, a present tense verb in the direct quote shifts to the imperfect indicative.

### Context
English: "Direct: 'I have a cold.' -> Indirect: 'She said she had a cold.'"

### Exercise
Directo: «Tengo frío». -> Indirecto: Ella dijo que (tener) ___ frío.

<!-- SOLUTION
tenía
-->
