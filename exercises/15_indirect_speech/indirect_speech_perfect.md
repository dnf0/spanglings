# Indirect Speech: Present Perfect to Pluperfect
<!-- id: c1_indirect_speech_perfect | level: C1 | topic: indirect_speech | type: transformation -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In indirect speech, if the reporting verb is in the past, a present perfect verb in the direct quote shifts to the pluperfect indicative.

### Context
English: "Direct: 'I have eaten.' -> Indirect: 'He said he had eaten.'"

### Exercise
Directo: «He comido». -> Indirecto: Dijo que (comer) ___ .

<!-- SOLUTION
había comido
