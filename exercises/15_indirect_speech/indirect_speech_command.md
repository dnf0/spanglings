# Indirect Speech: Command to Imperfect Subjunctive
<!-- id: c1_indirect_speech_command | level: C1 | topic: indirect_speech | type: transformation -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Direct commands (imperative) shift to the imperfect subjunctive in indirect speech when the reporting verb is in the past.

### Context
English: "Direct: 'Do it!' -> Indirect: 'He told me to do it.'"

### Exercise
Directo: «¡Hazlo!». -> Indirecto: Me dijo que lo (hacer) ___ .

<!-- SOLUTION
hiciera
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
hiciese
-->

<!-- DIAGNOSTIC_RULES
