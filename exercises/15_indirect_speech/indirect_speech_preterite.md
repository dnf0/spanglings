# Indirect Speech: Preterite to Pluperfect
<!-- id: c1_indirect_speech_preterite | level: C1 | topic: indirect_speech | type: transformation -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In indirect speech, if the reporting verb is in the past, a preterite verb in the direct quote shifts to the pluperfect indicative (había + past participle).

### Context
English: "Direct: 'I arrived yesterday.' -> Indirect: 'He said he had arrived the day before.'"

### Exercise
Directo: «Llegué ayer». -> Indirecto: Él dijo que (llegar) ___ el día anterior.

<!-- SOLUTION
había llegado
-->
