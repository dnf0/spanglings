> **Grammar Rule**: The structure '[Verb in Subjunctive] + lo que + [Same Verb in Subjunctive]' means 'no matter what you [verb]' (e.g. digas lo que digas, hagas lo que hagas).

### Context
English: "Whatever you do, don't sign the document."
