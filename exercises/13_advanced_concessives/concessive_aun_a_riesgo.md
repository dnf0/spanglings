# Concessive: Aun a riesgo de que
<!-- id: c1_concessive_aun_a_riesgo | level: C1 | topic: advanced_concessives | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Aun a riesgo de que' (even at the risk of) always triggers the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "Even at the risk of it being difficult, I will try."

### Exercise
Aun a riesgo de que (ser) ___ difícil, lo intentaré.

<!-- SOLUTION
sea
-->
