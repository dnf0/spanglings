# Concessive: Por mucho que
<!-- id: c1_concessive_por_mucho | level: C1 | topic: advanced_concessives | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Por mucho que' functions identically to 'por más que' and triggers the subjunctive for potential or unverified events.

### Context
English: "No matter how much you cry, you won't change my mind."

### Exercise
Por mucho que tú (llorar) ___ , no cambiaré de opinión.

<!-- SOLUTION
llores
-->
