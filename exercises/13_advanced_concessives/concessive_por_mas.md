# Concessive: Por más que
<!-- id: c1_concessive_por_mas | level: C1 | topic: advanced_concessives | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Por más que' triggers the subjunctive when referring to future or hypothetical obstacles, or to emphasize intense but unsuccessful effort.

### Context
English: "No matter how much he studies, he won't pass."

### Exercise
Por más que él (estudiar) ___ , no aprobará.

<!-- SOLUTION
estudie
