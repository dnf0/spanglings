# Concessive: A pesar de que
<!-- id: c1_concessive_a_pesar | level: C1 | topic: advanced_concessives | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'A pesar de que' takes the subjunctive when introducing a hypothetical, unverified, or irrelevant obstacle.

### Context
English: "Even though she might not like it (potential reaction), we will go."

### Exercise
A pesar de que no le (gustar) ___ , iremos.

<!-- SOLUTION
guste
