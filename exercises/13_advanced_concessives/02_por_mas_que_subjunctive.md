# Advanced Concessives 02: 'Por más que' + Subjunctive
<!-- id: c1_concessive_por_mas_que | level: C1 | topic: advanced_concessives | type: cloze | concepts: ["advanced_concessive_mood_alternation"] | prerequisites: ["subjunctive_adverbial_clauses", "hypothetical_conditionals_si_clauses"] | grammar_focus: "Advanced concessive clauses (por más que, aun a riesgo de) with mood alternation." | contrast_note: "Contrast factual concession (indicative) with hypothetical concession (subjunctive)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Por más que + Subjuntivo' conveys 'no matter how much / even if' when describing unfulfilled or hypothetical efforts.

### Context
English: "No matter how much you try, you will not convince him."

### Exercise
Por más que lo (intentar) ___, no lograrás convencerlo.

<!-- SOLUTION
intentes
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
