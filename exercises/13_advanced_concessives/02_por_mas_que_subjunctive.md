# Advanced Concessives 02: 'Por más que' + Subjunctive
<!-- id: c1_concessive_por_mas_que | level: C1 | topic: advanced_concessives | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Por más que + Subjuntivo' conveys 'no matter how much / even if' when describing unfulfilled or hypothetical efforts.

### Context
English: "No matter how much you try, you will not convince him."

### Exercise
Por más que lo (intentar) ___, no lograrás convencerlo.

<!-- SOLUTION
intentes
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
intentes tú
