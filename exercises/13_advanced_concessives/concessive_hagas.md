# Reduplicative Concessive Clauses
<!-- id: c1_concessive_hagas | level: C1 | topic: advanced_concessives | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Reduplicative concessive formulas (hagas lo que hagas, digas lo que digas) express 'no matter what you do/say' and always require the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "No matter what you do, I will support you."

### Exercise
___ lo que hagas, te apoyaré.

<!-- SOLUTION
Hagas
-->
