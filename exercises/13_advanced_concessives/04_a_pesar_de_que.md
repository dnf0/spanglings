# Advanced Concessives 04: 'A pesar de que' Mood Distinction
<!-- id: c1_concessive_a_pesar_de_que | level: C1 | topic: advanced_concessives | type: cloze | concepts: ["advanced_concessive_mood_alternation"] | prerequisites: ["subjunctive_adverbial_clauses", "hypothetical_conditionals_si_clauses"] | grammar_focus: "Advanced concessive clauses (por más que, aun a riesgo de) with mood alternation." | contrast_note: "Contrast factual concession (indicative) with hypothetical concession (subjunctive)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'A pesar de que' takes indicative for proven facts, but SUBJUNCTIVE when the fact is hypothetical, future, or dismissed as irrelevant by the speaker.

### Context
English: "Even if it rains tomorrow, we will carry out the outdoor event."

### Exercise
A pesar de que mañana (llover) ___, celebraremos el acto al aire libre.

<!-- SOLUTION
llueva
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
llueva mañana
