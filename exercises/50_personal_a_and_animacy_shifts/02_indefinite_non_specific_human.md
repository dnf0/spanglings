# Personal A 02: Indefinite / Generic Human Direct Objects
<!-- id: b1_personal_a_indefinite_human | level: B1 | topic: personal_a_and_animacy_shifts | type: cloze | concepts: ["personal_a_specificity_animacy"] | prerequisites: ["por_vs_para_foundations"] | grammar_focus: "Omitting personal 'a' when referring to an indefinite, non-specific profession or candidate." | contrast_note: "Indefinite generic candidate (*un especialista*) without 'a' vs specific individual with 'a'." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When seeking or hiring an unidentifiable, generic representative of a profession or category, personal 'a' is typically omitted (*Necesitamos contratar programador / un especialista*).

### Context
English: "The company urgently needs to hire an offensive cybersecurity specialist."

### Exercise
La empresa necesita contratar urgente (un especialista / a un especialista) ___ en ciberseguridad ofensiva.

<!-- SOLUTION
un especialista
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Un especialista
