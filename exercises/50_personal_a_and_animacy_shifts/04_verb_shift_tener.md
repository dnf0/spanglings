# Personal A 04: Semantic Shift with 'Tener' (Human Condition vs Possession)

> **Grammar Rule**: While *tener* normally excludes personal 'a' (*Tengo dos hermanos*), it REQUIRES personal 'a' when describing a human object in a temporary condition, state, or location (*Tiene a su socia hospitalizada*).

### Context
English: "The founder has his business partner hospitalized following the traffic accident."
