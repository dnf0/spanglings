# Personal A 06: Mandatory Personal A with 'Nadie' and 'Alguien'
<!-- id: b1_personal_a_nadie_alguien | level: B1 | topic: personal_a_and_animacy_shifts | type: cloze | concepts: ["personal_a_specificity_animacy"] | prerequisites: ["por_vs_para_foundations"] | grammar_focus: "Mandatory personal 'a' before human indefinite pronouns (alguien, nadie, quién)." | contrast_note: "Mandatory personal 'a' (*no encontramos a nadie*) vs omitting 'a'." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The indefinite pronouns *alguien, nadie, quién, ninguno/a* ALWAYS require personal 'a' when functioning as direct objects (*No vi a nadie*).

### Context
English: "When the security breach occurred on the server, we found nobody in the control room."

### Exercise
Cuando se produjo la brecha de seguridad en el servidor, no encontramos (a nadie / nadie) ___ en la sala de control.

<!-- SOLUTION
a nadie
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
A nadie
-->
