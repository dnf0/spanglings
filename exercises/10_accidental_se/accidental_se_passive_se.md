# Passive Se vs Impersonal Se
<!-- id: b2_accidental_se_passive_se | level: B2 | topic: accidental_se | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In passive 'se' constructions, the verb must agree in number with the inanimate patient noun.

### Context
English: "Apartments are rented."

### Exercise
Se ___ apartamentos.

<!-- SOLUTION
alquilan
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
rentan
-->

<!-- DIAGNOSTIC_RULES
pattern: "alquila" | code: "E1001" | message: "Since 'apartamentos' is plural, the verb must agree and be plural: 'alquilan'."
