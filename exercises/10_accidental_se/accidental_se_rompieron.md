# Accidental Se: Breaking
<!-- id: b2_accidental_se_rompieron | level: B2 | topic: accidental_se | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: For accidental breakage, the verb 'romper' must agree with the broken item(s) in gender and number.

### Context
English: "He broke his glasses (accidentally)."

### Exercise
Se le ___ los lentes.

<!-- SOLUTION
rompieron
