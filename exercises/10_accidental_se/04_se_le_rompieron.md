# Accidental Se 04: 'Se le rompieron' (He broke his glasses)
<!-- id: b2_accidental_se_rompieron | level: B2 | topic: accidental_se | type: cloze | concepts: ["accidental_se_structures"] | prerequisites: ["gustar_dative_structures", "clitic_pronoun_stacking"] | grammar_focus: "Involuntary and accidental 'se' construction for unplanned events." | contrast_note: "Contrast deliberate direct reflexive with accidental de-agentified se." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Use 'le' + plural verb when a 3rd person singular subject accidentally has plural items break.

### Context
English: "Carlos accidentally broke his glasses when playing basketball."

### Exercise
A Carlos se ___ (romper) las gafas jugando al baloncesto.

<!-- SOLUTION
le rompieron
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
le rompieron las gafas
-->

<!-- DIAGNOSTIC_RULES
pattern: "le rompió" | code: "E1001" | message: "'las gafas' is plural -> verb must be plural 'rompieron'."
-->
