# Accidental Se: Losing Things
<!-- id: b2_accidental_se_perdieron | level: B2 | topic: accidental_se | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Accidental loss uses 'se' + IOP + verb conjugated to agree with the plural objects lost.

### Context
English: "I lost my papers (accidentally)."

### Exercise
Se me (perder) ___ los papeles.

<!-- SOLUTION
perdieron
