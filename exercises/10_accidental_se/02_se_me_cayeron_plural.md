# Accidental Se 02: Plural Dropped Items
<!-- id: b2_accidental_se_plural | level: B2 | topic: accidental_se | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When the object dropped is plural, the verb MUST agree in plural with the grammatical subject.

### Context
English: "I accidentally dropped my keys."

### Exercise
Se ___ (caer) las llaves en la alcantarilla.

<!-- SOLUTION
me cayeron
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
me cayeron las llaves
-->

<!-- DIAGNOSTIC_RULES
pattern: "me cayó" | code: "E1001" | message: "'las llaves' is plural subject; verb must be plural: 'se me cayeron'."
-->

<!-- HINTS
