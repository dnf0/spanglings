# Accidental Se: Forgetting
<!-- id: b2_accidental_se_olvido | level: B2 | topic: accidental_se | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Unintentional forgetting uses 'se' + indirect pronoun (for the people who forgot) + verb conjugated to match the forgotten object(s).

### Context
English: "We forgot the keys (accidentally)."

### Exercise
Se ___ olvidaron las llaves.

<!-- SOLUTION
nos
