# Conditionals 03: Polite Requests with Imperfect Subjunctive
<!-- id: b2_cond_polite_quisiera | level: B2 | topic: conditionals | type: cloze | concepts: ["hypothetical_conditionals_si_clauses"] | prerequisites: ["imperfect_subjunctive_formation"] | grammar_focus: "Imperfect subjunctive in hypothetical and counterfactual 'si' clauses." | contrast_note: "Contrast real condition (present indicative) with hypothetical condition (imperfect subjunctive)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Quisiera' and 'pudiera' are used in polite requests to soften statements instead of direct indicative 'quiero' or 'puedo'.

### Context
English: "I would like to ask a question about the contract."

### Exercise
(Querer) ___ hacer una pregunta sobre las condiciones del contrato.

<!-- SOLUTION
quisiera
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
quisiese
querría
-->
