# Sequence of Tenses: Past Subjunctive
<!-- id: b2_imp_subj_cond_sequence | level: B2 | topic: imperfect_subjunctive_conditionals | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When the main clause verb is in the past (imperfect, preterite, conditional) and triggers the subjunctive, the dependent clause must use the imperfect subjunctive.

### Context
English: "I wanted them to help me."

### Exercise
Yo quería que ellos me (ayudar) ___ .

<!-- SOLUTION
ayudaran
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
ayudasen
-->
