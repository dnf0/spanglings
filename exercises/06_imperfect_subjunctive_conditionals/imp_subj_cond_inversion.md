# Conditional Sentences: Inversion
<!-- id: b2_imp_subj_cond_inversion | level: B2 | topic: imperfect_subjunctive_conditionals | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Even if the clauses are inverted (main clause first, then 'si' clause), the 'si' clause of contrary-to-fact conditions still requires the imperfect subjunctive.

### Context
English: "You would be happier if you slept more."

### Exercise
Estarías más feliz si tú (dormir) ___ más.

<!-- SOLUTION
durmieras
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
durmieses
-->
