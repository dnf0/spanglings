# Subjunctive: Polite Requests
<!-- id: b2_imp_subj_cond_polite | level: B2 | topic: imperfect_subjunctive_conditionals | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The imperfect subjunctive of 'querer' (quisiera) is widely used to make polite requests or express desires softly.

### Context
English: "I would like to ask you a favor."

### Exercise
(querer) ___ pedirte un favor.

<!-- SOLUTION
quisiera
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
quisiese
-->
