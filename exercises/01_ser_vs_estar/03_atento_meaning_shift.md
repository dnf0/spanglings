# Ser vs Estar 03: 'Atento' (Courteous vs Paying Attention)
<!-- id: b1_ser_estar_atento | level: B1 | topic: ser_vs_estar | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Ser atento' means polite and thoughtful; 'estar atento' means paying close attention.

### Context
English: "The students are paying attention to the teacher's explanation."

### Exercise
Los alumnos (estar) ___ muy atentos a la explicación del profesor.

<!-- SOLUTION
están
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
estan
-->
