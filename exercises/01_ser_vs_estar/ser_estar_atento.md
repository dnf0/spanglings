# Ser vs Estar: Atento
<!-- id: b1_ser_estar_atento | level: B1 | topic: ser_vs_estar | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Ser atento' means to be courteous, polite or thoughtful, whereas 'estar atento' means to be paying attention.

### Context
English: "Students must pay attention to the teacher's explanation."

### Exercise
Los estudiantes tienen que (estar) ___ atentos a la explicación del profesor.

<!-- SOLUTION
estar
-->
