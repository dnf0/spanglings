# Ser vs Estar: Listo
<!-- id: b1_ser_estar_listo | level: B1 | topic: ser_vs_estar | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: With adjectives, 'ser' and 'estar' change meaning. 'Ser listo' means to be clever/smart, whereas 'estar listo' means to be ready.

### Context
English: "Are you ready to go out?"

### Exercise
¿(estar) ___ listo para salir ya?

<!-- SOLUTION
estás
-->
