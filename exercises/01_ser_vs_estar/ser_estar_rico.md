# Ser vs Estar: Rico
<!-- id: b1_ser_estar_rico | level: B1 | topic: ser_vs_estar | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: For food, 'estar rico' means to taste delicious, whereas 'ser rico' means to be wealthy/rich.

### Context
English: "This cake is delicious; did you bake it?"

### Exercise
Este pastel (estar) ___ muy rico, ¿lo hiciste tú?

<!-- SOLUTION
está
