# Ser vs Estar 02: 'Rico' (Wealthy vs Delicious)
<!-- id: b1_ser_estar_rico | level: B1 | topic: ser_vs_estar | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Ser rico' means wealthy/affluent; 'estar rico' refers to delicious food or taste evaluation.

### Context
English: "This homemade soup is so delicious!"

### Exercise
¡Esta sopa casera (estar) ___ riquísima!

<!-- SOLUTION
está
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
esta
-->
