# Ser vs Estar 01: 'Listo' (Smart vs Ready)
<!-- id: b1_ser_estar_listo | level: B1 | topic: ser_vs_estar | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Ser listo' means to be clever/smart (inherent trait); 'estar listo' means to be ready or prepared (temporary state).

### Context
English: "We have finished packing and now we are ready to leave."

### Exercise
Ya hemos hecho las maletas y ahora (estar) ___ listos para salir.

<!-- SOLUTION
estamos
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
estamos listos
-->

<!-- DIAGNOSTIC_RULES
pattern: "somos" | code: "E0102" | message: "'somos listos' means 'we are clever'. To mean 'we are ready', use 'estamos listos'."
-->
