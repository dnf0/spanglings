# Ser vs Estar 04: 'Verde' (Color vs Unripe/Inexperienced)
<!-- id: b1_ser_estar_verde | level: B1 | topic: ser_vs_estar | type: cloze | concepts: ["ser_vs_estar_adjectival_shifts"] | prerequisites: ["ser_vs_estar_essence_state"] | grammar_focus: "Adjectival meaning shift depending on ser vs estar." | contrast_note: "Contrast permanent trait (ser) with temporary state (estar)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Ser verde' refers to green color or eco-friendly; 'estar verde' means unripe (fruit) or inexperienced (person/project).

### Context
English: "Don't eat those bananas yet; they are still unripe."

### Exercise
No comas esos plátanos todavía; todavía (estar) ___ verdes.

<!-- SOLUTION
están
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
estan
-->
