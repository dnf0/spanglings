# Ser vs Estar: Verde
<!-- id: b1_ser_estar_verde | level: B1 | topic: ser_vs_estar | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: For fruit and plants, 'estar verde' means to be unripe, whereas 'ser verde' means the object has a green color.

### Context
English: "The bananas are still green; don't eat them."

### Exercise
Los plátanos todavía (estar) ___ verdes, no los comas.

<!-- SOLUTION
están
