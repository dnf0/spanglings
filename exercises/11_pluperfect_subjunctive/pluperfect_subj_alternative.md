# Pluperfect Subjunctive: Alternative in Main Clause
<!-- id: c1_pluperfect_subj_alternative | level: C1 | topic: pluperfect_subjunctive | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In literary or formal Spanish, the '-ra' form of the pluperfect subjunctive (e.g. hubiera) can optionally be used in the main clause instead of the conditional perfect.

### Context
English: "If you had asked me, I would have given it to you."

### Exercise
Si me lo hubieras pedido, te lo ___ dado.

<!-- SOLUTION
habría
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
hubiera
-->
