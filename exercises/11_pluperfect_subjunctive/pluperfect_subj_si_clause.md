# Pluperfect Subjunctive: Si Clause
<!-- id: c1_pluperfect_subj_si_clause | level: C1 | topic: pluperfect_subjunctive | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In past contrary-to-fact conditional sentences, the 'si' clause uses the pluperfect subjunctive (hubiera/hubiese + past participle) and the main clause uses the conditional perfect.

### Context
English: "If you had told me, I would have gone with you."

### Exercise
Si tú me ___ dicho la verdad, habría ido contigo.

<!-- SOLUTION
hubieras
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
hubieses
-->
