# Pluperfect Subjunctive: Ojalá
<!-- id: c1_pluperfect_subj_ojala | level: C1 | topic: pluperfect_subjunctive | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Ojalá' with the pluperfect subjunctive is used to express deep regret or wishes about past events that cannot be changed.

### Context
English: "I wish I had known earlier."

### Exercise
Ojalá lo ___ sabido antes.

<!-- SOLUTION
hubiera
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
hubiese
-->
