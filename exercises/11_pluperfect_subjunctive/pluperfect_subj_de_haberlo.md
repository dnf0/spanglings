# Pluperfect Subjunctive: De Haberlo
<!-- id: c1_pluperfect_subj_de_haberlo | level: C1 | topic: pluperfect_subjunctive | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The structure 'de haber + past participle' is a formal contraction equivalent to a hypothetical 'si' clause (meaning 'if I had...').

### Context
English: "Had I known it, I wouldn't have come."

### Exercise
De haberlo (saber) ___ , no habría venido.

<!-- SOLUTION
sabido
