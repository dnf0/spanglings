# Epistemic Conjecture 04: Past Age & Quantity Approximation (Tener)
<!-- id: b2_conjecture_past_age_tener | level: B2 | topic: epistemic_conjecture_and_probability | type: cloze | concepts: ["conditional_of_probability_past"] | prerequisites: ["future_of_probability_present", "imperfect_aspect_habitual_background"] | grammar_focus: "Approximating age, quantity, or time in the past with the conditional of 'tener'." | contrast_note: "Conditional (*tendría*) expressing estimated past age vs imperfect (*tenía*)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Use *tendría* to estimate an approximate past age or amount without making a factual assertion.

### Context
English: "When he founded his first tech company, he was probably barely twenty-five years old."

### Exercise
Cuando fundó su primera empresa de tecnología, (tendría / tenía probablemente) ___ apenas veinticinco años.

<!-- SOLUTION
tendría
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Tendría
-->
