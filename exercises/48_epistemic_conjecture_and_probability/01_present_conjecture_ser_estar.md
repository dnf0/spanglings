# Epistemic Conjecture 01: Present Probability with Future (Ser)
<!-- id: b2_conjecture_ser_present | level: B2 | topic: epistemic_conjecture_and_probability | type: cloze | concepts: ["future_of_probability_present"] | prerequisites: ["irregular_present_stems"] | grammar_focus: "Expressing present conjecture and probability using the simple future tense." | contrast_note: "Present future (*serán*) expressing probability ('must be / is probably') vs present indicative (*son*)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In Spanish, the simple future tense (*futuro simple*) expresses conjecture or probability about the present state (*¿Qué hora es? -> Serán las cuatro* = 'It is probably around four').

### Context
English: "What time is it? I don't have a watch, but it's probably around 4:00 PM."

### Exercise
¿Qué hora es? No tengo reloj, pero (serán / son probablemente) ___ las cuatro de la tarde.

<!-- SOLUTION
serán
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Serán
-->
