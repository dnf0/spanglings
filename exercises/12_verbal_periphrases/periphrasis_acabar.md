# Verbal Periphrasis: Acabar de + Infinitive
<!-- id: c1_periphrasis_acabar | level: C1 | topic: verbal_periphrases | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The periphrasis 'acabar de + infinitive' indicates that an action has just been completed in the very recent past.

### Context
English: "I have just eaten a minute ago."

### Exercise
Acabo ___ comer hace un minuto.

<!-- SOLUTION
de
