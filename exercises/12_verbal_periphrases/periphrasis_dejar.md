# Verbal Periphrasis: Dejar de + Infinitive
<!-- id: c1_periphrasis_dejar | level: C1 | topic: verbal_periphrases | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The periphrasis 'dejar de + infinitive' indicates the cessation of an action (stopping an activity).

### Context
English: "She stopped smoking last year."

### Exercise
Ella dejó ___ fumar el año pasado.

<!-- SOLUTION
de
