# Verbal Periphrasis: Llevar + Gerundio
<!-- id: c1_periphrasis_llevar | level: C1 | topic: verbal_periphrases | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The periphrasis 'llevar + gerundio' describes an action that started in the past and continues in the present, indicating how long it has been going on.

### Context
English: "I have been living in Madrid for three years."

### Exercise
___ tres años viviendo en Madrid.

<!-- SOLUTION
Llevo
-->
