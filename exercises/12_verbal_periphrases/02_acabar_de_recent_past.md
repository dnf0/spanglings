# Verbal Periphrases 02: 'Acabar de + Inf' (Immediate Past)
<!-- id: b2_periphrasis_acabar_de | level: B2 | topic: verbal_periphrases | type: cloze | concepts: ["verbal_periphrases_aspect"] | prerequisites: ["preterite_aspect_completion", "imperfect_aspect_habitual_background"] | grammar_focus: "Verbal periphrases of inception, duration, repetition, and culmination." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Acabar de + infinitivo' expresses an action that occurred just moments ago.

### Context
English: "We have just arrived at the airport."

### Exercise
Nosotros (acabar) ___ de llegar al aeropuerto.

<!-- SOLUTION
acabamos
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
acabamos nosotros
-->
