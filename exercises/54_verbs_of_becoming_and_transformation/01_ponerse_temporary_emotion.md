# Verbs of Becoming 01: Ponerse (Temporary Emotional Reaction)
<!-- id: b2_becoming_ponerse_emotion | level: B2 | topic: verbs_of_becoming_and_transformation | type: cloze | concepts: ["becoming_temporary_state_ponerse", "irregular_preterite_stems"] | prerequisites: ["ser_vs_estar_adjectival_shifts"] | grammar_focus: "Involuntary and rapid transition into a temporary emotional state using ponerse + adjective." | contrast_note: "'Ponerse furioso' (temporary emotional state) vs invalid '*hacerse furioso' vs 'volverse furioso' (permanent character shift)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: *Ponerse* + adjective expresses a sudden, involuntary, and temporary change in emotional or psychological state (e.g., *ponerse contento, ponerse furioso*).

### Context
English: "When the deployment failed at midnight, the infrastructure director became furious."

### Exercise
Cuando el despliegue falló a medianoche, el director de infraestructura (se puso / se hizo) ___ furioso.

<!-- SOLUTION
se puso
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Se puso
-->
