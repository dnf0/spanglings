# Verbs of Becoming 02: Ponerse (Involuntary Physical Reaction)

> **Grammar Rule**: Bodily and physiological changes outside voluntary control (blushing, turning pale, falling ill) use *ponerse* (e.g. *ponerse rojo, ponerse pálido, ponerse enfermo*).

### Context
English: "During the presentation before the executive board, I turned completely red from nervousness."
