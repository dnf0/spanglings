# Verbs of Becoming 03: Quedarse (Resulting State & Reaction)
<!-- id: b2_becoming_quedarse_shock | level: B2 | topic: verbs_of_becoming_and_transformation | type: cloze | concepts: ["becoming_resulting_state_quedarse"] | prerequisites: ["becoming_temporary_state_ponerse"] | grammar_focus: "Resulting state of astonishment, shock, or deprivation caused by an external event using quedarse." | contrast_note: "'Quedarse atónito' (resulting aftermath of a shock) vs invalid '*ponerse atónito'." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: *Quedarse* + adjective emphasizes the resulting aftermath, shock, or state of loss/deprivation caused by an external catalyst (*quedarse atónito, quedarse sin batería, quedarse ciego*).

### Context
English: "Upon seeing the unexpected drop in performance metrics, we were left stunned."

### Exercise
Al ver la caída inesperada de las métricas de rendimiento, nosotros (nos quedamos / nos hicimos) ___ atónitos.

<!-- SOLUTION
nos quedamos
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Nos quedamos
-->
