# Verbs of Becoming 06: Convertirse En (Radical Metamorphosis)
<!-- id: c1_becoming_convertirse_radical | level: C1 | topic: verbs_of_becoming_and_transformation | type: cloze | concepts: ["becoming_radical_transformation_convertirse_en", "becoming_achievement_culmination_llegar_a_ser"] | prerequisites: ["prepositional_regimes"] | grammar_focus: "Radical transformation into a new category or state using convertirse en + noun." | contrast_note: "'Convertirse en un referente' (categorical metamorphosis) vs 'hacerse un referente' (less formal)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: *Convertirse en* + noun represents a complete transformation or metamorphosis into a new ontological category (*convertirse en un líder del sector*).

### Context
English: "With persistence and leadership, the small startup became a global benchmark in the sector."

### Exercise
Con persistencia y liderazgo, la pequeña empresa emergente (se convirtió / se puso) ___ en un referente global del sector.

<!-- SOLUTION
se convirtió
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Se convirtió
-->
