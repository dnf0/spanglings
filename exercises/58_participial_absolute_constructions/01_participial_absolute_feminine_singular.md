> **Grammar Rule**: In absolute participial constructions (*construcciones absolutas de participio*), the participle agrees in gender and number with the following subject (*Concluida la sesión...* = *Una vez concluida la sesión...*).

### Context
English: "The external cybersecurity audit having concluded, the executive committee proceeded to approve the renewal budget."
