# Pronoun Stacking: Negative Commands
<!-- id: b2_pronoun_stacking_negation | level: B2 | topic: pronoun_stacking | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: For negative commands, pronouns must precede the verb and remain separated (no attachment).

### Context
English: "Don't give it to me."

### Exercise
No ___ des.

<!-- SOLUTION
me lo
-->
