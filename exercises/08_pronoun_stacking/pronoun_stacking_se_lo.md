# Pronoun Stacking: Se Lo
<!-- id: b2_pronoun_stacking_se_lo | level: B2 | topic: pronoun_stacking | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When third-person indirect (le/les) and direct (lo/la/los/las) pronouns are combined, 'le/les' changes to 'se'.

### Context
English: "I told it to him yesterday."

### Exercise
___ dije ayer.

<!-- SOLUTION
se lo
-->
