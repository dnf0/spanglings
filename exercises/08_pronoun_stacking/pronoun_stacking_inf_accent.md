# Pronoun Stacking: Infinitive Accentuation
<!-- id: b2_pronoun_stacking_inf_accent | level: B2 | topic: pronoun_stacking | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When two pronouns are attached to an infinitive, a written accent is required on the vowel of the infinitive stem to maintain correct stress.

### Context
English: "I need to tell it to him today."

### Exercise
Necesito (decir + se + lo) ___ hoy.

<!-- SOLUTION
decírselo
