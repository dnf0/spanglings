# Pronoun Stacking: Gerund Accentuation
<!-- id: b2_pronoun_stacking_gerund_accent | level: B2 | topic: pronoun_stacking | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When pronouns are attached to a gerund (ending in -ando or -iendo), a written accent is required on the stressed vowel of the gerund ending.

### Context
English: "He is explaining it to me right now."

### Exercise
Él está (explicando + me + lo) ___ ahora.

<!-- SOLUTION
explicándomelo
