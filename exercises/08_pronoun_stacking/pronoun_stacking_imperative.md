# Pronoun Stacking: Imperative
<!-- id: b2_pronoun_stacking_imperative | level: B2 | topic: pronoun_stacking | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: For affirmative commands, pronouns must be attached directly to the end of the verb. Written accents are added to maintain the stress on the verb.

### Context
English: "Tell it to me now! (informal command)"

### Exercise
¡ (decir + me + lo) ___ ya!

<!-- SOLUTION
dímelo
-->
