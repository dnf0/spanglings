# Cleft Sentences: Lo que pasa es que
<!-- id: c1_cleft_lo_que_pasa | level: C1 | topic: cleft_sentences | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Lo que pasa es que' is a cleft-like discourse formula used to introduce an explanation, excuse, or justification.

### Context
English: "The thing is (what happens is that) I don't have time."

### Exercise
Lo que ___ es que no tengo tiempo.

<!-- SOLUTION
pasa
