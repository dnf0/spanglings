# Cleft Sentences 02: Explanatory Cleft ('Lo que pasa es que')
<!-- id: c1_cleft_lo_que_pasa | level: C1 | topic: cleft_sentences | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Lo que pasa es que...' is a ubiquitous explanatory discourse marker in native Spanish.

### Context
English: "The thing is that I don't have sufficient information."

### Exercise
Lo que ___ es que no dispongo de la información necesaria.

<!-- SOLUTION
pasa
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
pasa es
-->
