# Optatives & Legal Tenses 01: Counterfactual Longing with 'Quién'
<!-- id: c1_optative_quien_pudiera | level: C1 | topic: independent_subjunctives_and_legal_tenses | type: cloze | concepts: ["optative_independent_subjunctive"] | prerequisites: ["imperfect_subjunctive_formation", "subjunctive_volition_influence"] | grammar_focus: "Expressing unattainable yearning and counterfactual desires with '¡Quién + imperfecto de subjuntivo!'." | contrast_note: "Counterfactual optative (*¡Quién pudiera!*) vs factual indicative." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The exclamatory formula *¡Quién + imperfecto de subjuntivo!* expresses an impossible or unattainable personal wish (*¡Quién tuviera 20 años!* / *¡Quién pudiera...!*).

### Context
English: "If only one could debug concurrent distributed systems with such ease!"

### Exercise
¡(Quién pudiera / Quién puede) ___ depurar sistemas distribuidos concurrentes con tanta facilidad!

<!-- SOLUTION
Quién pudiera
-->
