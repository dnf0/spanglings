# Optatives & Legal Tenses 04: Future Subjunctive in Contractual Breaches
<!-- id: c1_legal_future_subjunctive_incumpliere | level: C1 | topic: independent_subjunctives_and_legal_tenses | type: cloze | concepts: ["future_subjunctive_legal_archaic"] | prerequisites: ["imperfect_subjunctive_formation"] | grammar_focus: "Forming the legal future subjunctive (-iere) in conditional contract clauses." | contrast_note: "Future subjunctive (*incumpliere*) in legal statutory drafting vs future indicative (*incumplirá*)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Legal contracts and statutory clauses employ the future subjunctive (*incumpliere, dispusiere, cometiere*) for hypothetical breach contingencies.

### Context
English: "If either signatory party should breach the stipulated confidentiality obligations, it shall incur criminal penalties."

### Exercise
Si alguna de las partes firmantes (incumpliere / incumplirá) ___ las obligaciones de confidencialidad estipuladas, incurrirá en penalización penal.

<!-- SOLUTION
incumpliere
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Incumpliere
-->
