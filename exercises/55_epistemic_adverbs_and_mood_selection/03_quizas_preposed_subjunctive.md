# Epistemic Adverbs 03: Quizás (Preposed Subjunctive of Uncertainty)

> **Grammar Rule**: When *quizás* or *tal vez* precedes the verb, the SUBJUNCTIVE is used when the speaker intends to emphasize uncertainty or doubt (*Quizás sea conveniente*).

### Context
