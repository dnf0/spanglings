> **Grammar Rule**: In rhetorical questions expressing incredulity or challenging an assumption, *acaso* takes the indicative mood (*¿Acaso pensáis que es fácil?*).

### Context
English: "Do you really think that a complete cloud migration is accomplished without hitches?"
