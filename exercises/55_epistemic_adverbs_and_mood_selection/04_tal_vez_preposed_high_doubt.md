> **Grammar Rule**: Pre-verbal *tal vez* introduces hypothetical doubt and takes the present subjunctive (*Tal vez encuentren fallos*).

### Context
English: "Perhaps the auditors may find inconsistencies in the records if we do not normalize the schema."
