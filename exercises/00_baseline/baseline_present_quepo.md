# Irregular Present Stem: Caber
<!-- id: baseline_present_quepo | level: Baseline | topic: baseline | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Caber (to fit) has a highly irregular 'yo' form in the present indicative: 'quepo'.

### Context
English: "I don't fit in this small car."

### Exercise
Yo no (caber) ___ en este coche tan pequeño.

<!-- SOLUTION
quepo
-->
