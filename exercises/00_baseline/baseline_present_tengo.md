# Irregular Present Stem: Tener
<!-- id: baseline_present_tengo | level: Baseline | topic: baseline | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Tener (to have) is irregular in the 'yo' form of the present tense: 'tengo'.

### Context
English: "I have a lot of books in my room."

### Exercise
Yo (tener) ___ muchos libros en mi habitación.

<!-- SOLUTION
tengo
