# Adversatives 04: Finite Clause Substitution with 'Sino Que'
<!-- id: b2_adversative_sino_que_finite_clause | level: B2 | topic: adversative_pero_sino_sino_que | type: cloze | concepts: ["adversative_sino_que_clauses"] | prerequisites: ["adversative_pero_vs_sino"] | grammar_focus: "Using 'sino que' when exclusive substitution introduces a full clause with a finite conjugated verb." | contrast_note: "Finite clause substitution (*sino que rediseñó*) vs bare *sino* or *pero*." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When substituting a negative premise with a full finite verb clause, *sino que* is grammatically required (*No solo corregimos... sino que rediseñamos...*).

### Context
English: "The team didn't just fix the error in production, but rather redesigned the entire authentication subsystem."

### Exercise
El equipo no solo corrigió el error en producción, (sino que rediseñó / pero rediseñó) ___ todo el subsistema de autenticación.

<!-- SOLUTION
sino que rediseñó
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Sino que rediseñó
-->
