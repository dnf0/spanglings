# Formal Inversion 02: Absolute Participle Clause
<!-- id: c1_formal_llegado_el_momento | level: C1 | topic: formal_inversion | type: cloze | concepts: ["formal_inversion_hyperbaton"] | prerequisites: ["cleft_sentences_focus"] | grammar_focus: "Stylistic syntactic inversion, fronting, and rhetorical word order." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Absolute participle clauses place the participle first, agreeing with the following noun (e.g. 'Llegado el momento...', 'Terminada la reunión...').

### Context
English: "When the moment arrived, we made the final decision."

### Exercise
___ el momento oportuno, tomaremos la decisión definitiva.

<!-- SOLUTION
Llegado
-->
