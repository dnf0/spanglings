# Absolute Clause: Una vez + Participle
<!-- id: c1_formal_inversion_una_vez | level: C1 | topic: formal_inversion | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In absolute clauses starting with 'una vez' (once / once finished), the past participle must agree in gender and number with the following noun.

### Context
English: "Once the meeting was finished, everyone left."

### Exercise
Una vez (terminar) ___ la reunión, todos se fueron.

<!-- SOLUTION
terminada
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
concluida
-->
