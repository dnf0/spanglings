# Formal Subjunctive: El hecho de que
<!-- id: c1_formal_inversion_hecho_que | level: C1 | topic: formal_inversion | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'El hecho de que' (the fact that) triggers the subjunctive when the clause functions as a concession or is treated with distance, doubt, or rejection.

### Context
English: "The fact that he is young doesn't mean he is inexperienced."

### Exercise
El hecho de que él (ser) ___ joven no significa que no tenga experiencia.

<!-- SOLUTION
sea
-->
