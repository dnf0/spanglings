# Absolute Clause: Llegado el momento
<!-- id: c1_formal_inversion_llegado | level: C1 | topic: formal_inversion | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: An absolute participle clause is constructed by placing the past participle first, which must agree in gender and number with the noun that follows it.

### Context
English: "Once the moment arrived, we acted."

### Exercise
___ el momento, actuamos.

<!-- SOLUTION
Llegado
-->
