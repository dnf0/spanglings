# Absolute Clause: Habiendo + Participle
<!-- id: c1_formal_inversion_habiendo | level: C1 | topic: formal_inversion | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: A compound gerund absolute clause is formed using 'Habiendo + past participle' to mean 'Having [done something]'.

### Context
English: "Having considered all options, we decided to accept."

### Exercise
___ considerado todas las opciones, decidimos aceptar.

<!-- SOLUTION
Habiendo
