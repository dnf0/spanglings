# Subjunctive WEIRDO 01: Verbs of Wishing & Volition
<!-- id: b1_subj_weirdo_wishes | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_weirdo | type: cloze | concepts: ["subjunctive_volition_influence"] | prerequisites: ["irregular_present_stems", "irregular_subjunctive_stems"] | grammar_focus: "Subjunctive mood selection in subordinate clauses of influence and volition." | contrast_note: "Contrast indicative certainty with subjunctive volition/doubt." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Verbs expressing desire, demand, or wish (querer, desear, exigir, pedir) trigger the subjunctive when there is a change of subject across clauses.

### Context
English: "My parents want me to study law."

### Exercise
Mis padres quieren que yo (estudiar) ___ Derecho.

<!-- SOLUTION
estudie
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
estudie yo
-->
