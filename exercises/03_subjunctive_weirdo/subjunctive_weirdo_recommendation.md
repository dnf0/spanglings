# Subjunctive: Recommendation
<!-- id: b1_subjunctive_weirdo_recommendation | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_weirdo | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Verbs of recommendation, request, or advice (aconsejar, recomendar, sugerir) trigger the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "I advise you to eat more fruit."

### Exercise
Te aconsejo que (comer) ___ más fruta.

<!-- SOLUTION
comas
