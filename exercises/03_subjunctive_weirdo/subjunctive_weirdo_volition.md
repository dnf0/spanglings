# Subjunctive: Volition
<!-- id: b1_subjunctive_weirdo_volition | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_weirdo | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Verbs of wishing, wanting, or influence (querer, desear, esperar) trigger the subjunctive in the subordinate clause when there is a change of subject.

### Context
English: "I want you to come with me."

### Exercise
Quiero que tú (venir) ___ conmigo.

<!-- SOLUTION
vengas
-->
