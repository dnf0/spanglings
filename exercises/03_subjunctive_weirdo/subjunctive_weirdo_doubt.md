# Subjunctive: Doubt
<!-- id: b1_subjunctive_weirdo_doubt | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_weirdo | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Expressions of doubt or denial (dudar, no creer, no pensar) trigger the subjunctive in the dependent clause.

### Context
English: "I don't think he knows the answer."

### Exercise
No creo que él (saber) ___ la respuesta.

<!-- SOLUTION
sepa
