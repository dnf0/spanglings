# Subjunctive WEIRDO 03: Impersonal Value Judgments
<!-- id: b1_subj_weirdo_impersonal | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_weirdo | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Impersonal phrases stating necessity, importance, or value judgment (es necesario que, es importante que, es bueno que) trigger subjunctive.

### Context
English: "It is necessary that we speak with the manager."

### Exercise
Es necesario que nosotros (hablar) ___ con el gerente.

<!-- SOLUTION
hablemos
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
hablemos nosotros
-->

<!-- DIAGNOSTIC_RULES
pattern: "hablamos" | code: "E0301" | message: "For -ar verbs, 1st person plural subjunctive ending is '-emos' ('hablemos')."
