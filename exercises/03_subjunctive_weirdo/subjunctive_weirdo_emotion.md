# Subjunctive: Emotion
<!-- id: b1_subjunctive_weirdo_emotion | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_weirdo | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Verbs or expressions of emotion (alegrarse de, molestar, gustar) trigger the subjunctive in the dependent clause.

### Context
English: "I'm glad you are here."

### Exercise
Me alegra que tú (estar) ___ aquí.

<!-- SOLUTION
estés
