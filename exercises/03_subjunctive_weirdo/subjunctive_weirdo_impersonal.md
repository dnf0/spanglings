# Subjunctive: Impersonal
<!-- id: b1_subjunctive_weirdo_impersonal | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_weirdo | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Impersonal expressions that do not state a objective fact (es necesario que, es importante que) trigger the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "It is necessary that you study for the exam."

### Exercise
Es necesario que tú (estudiar) ___ para el examen.

<!-- SOLUTION
estudies
-->
