# Subjunctive: Ojalá
<!-- id: b1_subjunctive_weirdo_ojala | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_weirdo | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Ojalá (que)' always triggers the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "I hope it doesn't rain tomorrow."

### Exercise
Ojalá no (llover) ___ mañana.

<!-- SOLUTION
llueva
