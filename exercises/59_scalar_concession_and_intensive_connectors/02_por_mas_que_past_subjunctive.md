> **Grammar Rule**: In past narratives where efforts yielded no result, *por más que* governs the imperfect subjunctive (*Por más que investigaran, no hallaron nada*).

### Context
English: "No matter how much security analysts investigated the network traces for days, they found no traces of external intrusion."
