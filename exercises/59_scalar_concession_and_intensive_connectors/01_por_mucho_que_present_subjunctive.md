# Scalar Concession 01: Por Mucho Que (Present Subjunctive)

> **Grammar Rule**: *Por mucho que* expresses scalar concession ('no matter how much...') and strictly governs the SUBJUNCTIVE mood (*Por mucho que intenten convencerme...*).

### Context
English: "No matter how much competitors try to accelerate their deadlines, we will not compromise code security."
