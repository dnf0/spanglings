# Intensive Concession 04: Aun A Riesgo De Que (Hypothetical Danger)

> **Grammar Rule**: *Aun a riesgo de que* ('even at the risk of...') introduces a hypothetical downside and takes the SUBJUNCTIVE (*Aun a riesgo de que surjan problemas...*).

### Context
English: "We will deploy the patch tonight, even at the risk that minor alerts arise in the monitoring dashboard."
