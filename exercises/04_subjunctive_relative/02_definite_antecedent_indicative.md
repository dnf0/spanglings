# Subjunctive in Relative Clauses 02: Known/Definite Antecedent
<!-- id: b1_subj_relative_definite | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_relative | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When the antecedent is known, specific, and confirmed to exist (definite article 'el/la'), use the indicative mood.

### Context
English: "I bought the apartment that has views of the sea."

### Exercise
Compré el piso que (tener) ___ vistas al mar.

<!-- SOLUTION
tiene
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
tiene el piso
-->
