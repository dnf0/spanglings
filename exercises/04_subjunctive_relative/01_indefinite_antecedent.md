# Subjunctive in Relative Clauses 01: Indefinite Antecedent
<!-- id: b1_subj_relative_indefinite | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_relative | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When a relative clause modifies an antecedent that is unknown, unspecific, or hypothetical, use the subjunctive mood.

### Context
English: "I am looking for an apartment that has three bedrooms."

### Exercise
Busco un piso que (tener) ___ tres dormitorios.

<!-- SOLUTION
tenga
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
tenga el piso
-->
