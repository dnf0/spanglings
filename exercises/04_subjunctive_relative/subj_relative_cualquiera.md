# Subjunctive: Cualquiera
<!-- id: b2_subj_relative_cualquiera | level: B2 | topic: subjunctive_relative | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Relative clauses starting with 'cualquiera que' (whoever / anyone who) always require the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "Anyone who wants to can participate."

### Exercise
Cualquiera que (querer) ___ puede participar.

<!-- SOLUTION
quiera
-->
