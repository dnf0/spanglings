# Subjunctive: Negative Antecedent
<!-- id: b2_subj_relative_negative | level: B2 | topic: subjunctive_relative | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: If the antecedent is negated (nadie, nada, ninguno), the relative clause must be in the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "There is no one who knows the solution."

### Exercise
No hay nadie que (saber) ___ la solución.

<!-- SOLUTION
sepa
-->
