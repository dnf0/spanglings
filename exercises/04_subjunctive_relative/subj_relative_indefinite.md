# Subjunctive: Indefinite Antecedent
<!-- id: b2_subj_relative_indefinite | level: B2 | topic: subjunctive_relative | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Relative clauses use the subjunctive if they describe an indefinite, unknown, or hypothetical antecedent.

### Context
English: "I am looking for a book that has good illustrations (any book)."

### Exercise
Busco un libro que (tener) ___ buenas ilustraciones.

<!-- SOLUTION
tenga
-->
