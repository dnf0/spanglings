# Subjunctive: Unknown Person
<!-- id: b2_subj_relative_unknown_person | level: B2 | topic: subjunctive_relative | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Adjective/relative clauses referring to a person whose identity is unknown or hypothetical use the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "We need an employee who speaks French."

### Exercise
Necesitamos un empleado que (hablar) ___ francés.

<!-- SOLUTION
hable
-->
