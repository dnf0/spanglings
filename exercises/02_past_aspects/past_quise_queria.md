# Querer: Preterite vs Imperfect
<!-- id: b1_past_quise_queria | level: B1 | topic: past_aspects | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In the negative, 'no querer' in preterite means 'refused' (a specific act of refusal), whereas in the imperfect it means 'did not want' (ongoing mental state).

### Context
English: "She refused to talk to me yesterday."

### Exercise
Ayer ella no (querer) ___ hablar conmigo.

<!-- SOLUTION
quiso
