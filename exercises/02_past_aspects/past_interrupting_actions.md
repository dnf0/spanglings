# Interrupting Past Actions
<!-- id: b1_past_interrupting_actions | level: B1 | topic: past_aspects | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When an ongoing action in the past (imperfect) is interrupted, the interrupting action is conjugated in the preterite.

### Context
English: "I was sleeping when the door broke (suddenly)."

### Exercise
Yo dormía cuando se (romper) ___ la puerta.

<!-- SOLUTION
rompió
