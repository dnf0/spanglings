# Preterite vs Imperfect: Background
<!-- id: b1_past_preterite_imperfect_aspect | level: B1 | topic: past_aspects | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The imperfect describes ongoing background actions in the past, while the preterite describes completed actions that interrupt or occur.

### Context
English: "While I was studying, the phone rang."

### Exercise
Mientras yo (estudiar) ___ , el teléfono sonó.

<!-- SOLUTION
estudiaba
