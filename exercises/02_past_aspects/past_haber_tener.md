# Haber vs Tener in Past
<!-- id: b1_past_haber_tener | level: B1 | topic: past_aspects | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Haber' in the past imperfect ('había') expresses existence (there was/were), while 'tener' expresses possession (had).

### Context
English: "There were many people at the concert."

### Exercise
(haber) ___ mucha gente en el concierto.

<!-- SOLUTION
había
-->
