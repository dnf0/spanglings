# Poder: Preterite vs Imperfect
<!-- id: b1_past_pude_podia | level: B1 | topic: past_aspects | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In the preterite, 'poder' means to succeed in doing / managing to do something. In the imperfect, 'podía' means was capable of doing it (ongoing ability).

### Context
English: "After trying for hours, I managed to open the box."

### Exercise
Después de intentarlo por horas, yo (poder) ___ abrir la caja.

<!-- SOLUTION
pude
