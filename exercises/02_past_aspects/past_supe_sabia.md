# Saber: Preterite vs Imperfect
<!-- id: b1_past_supe_sabia | level: B1 | topic: past_aspects | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In the preterite, 'saber' means to find out or discover information. In the imperfect, it means to know (already have knowledge).

### Context
English: "Yesterday I found out the truth."

### Exercise
Ayer yo (saber) ___ la verdad.

<!-- SOLUTION
supe
