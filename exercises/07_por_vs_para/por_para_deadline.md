# Por vs Para: Deadline
<!-- id: b1_por_para_deadline | level: B1 | topic: por_vs_para | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Para' is used to express deadlines or specific future times, whereas 'por' is used for duration.

### Context
English: "The homework is due by Monday."

### Exercise
La tarea es ___ el lunes.

<!-- SOLUTION
para
-->
