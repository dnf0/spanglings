# Por vs Para 05: Means, Medium & Route (Por)
<!-- id: b1_por_para_medium | level: B1 | topic: por_vs_para | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Use 'por' for means of communication, transit route (through/along), or medium.

### Context
English: "I sent you the confidential document by email."

### Exercise
Te envié el documento confidencial ___ correo electrónico.

<!-- SOLUTION
por
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
por
