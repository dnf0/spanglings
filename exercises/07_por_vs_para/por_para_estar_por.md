# Estar Por vs Estar Para
<!-- id: b1_por_para_estar_por | level: B2 | topic: por_vs_para | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Estar para' indicates that something is about to happen or ready to happen. 'Estar por' indicates inclination (feeling like doing something) or that something is yet to be done.

### Context
English: "I am about to leave (ready to walk out the door)."

### Exercise
Estoy ___ salir.

<!-- SOLUTION
para
