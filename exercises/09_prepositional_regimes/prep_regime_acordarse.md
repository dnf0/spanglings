# Prepositional Regimes: Acordarse
<!-- id: b2_prep_regime_acordarse | level: B2 | topic: prepositional_regimes | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The pronominal verb 'acordarse' (to remember) requires the preposition 'de' (acordarse de algo), whereas 'recordar' is transitive (recordar algo).

### Context
English: "I don't remember his name."

### Exercise
No me acuerdo ___ su nombre.

<!-- SOLUTION
de
