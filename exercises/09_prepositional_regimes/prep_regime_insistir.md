# Prepositional Regimes: Insistir
<!-- id: b2_prep_regime_insistir | level: B2 | topic: prepositional_regimes | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The verb 'insistir' (to insist on) requires the preposition 'en' in Spanish.

### Context
English: "He insisted on paying the bill."

### Exercise
Él insistió ___ pagar la cuenta.

<!-- SOLUTION
en
-->
