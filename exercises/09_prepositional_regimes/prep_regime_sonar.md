# Prepositional Regimes: Soñar
<!-- id: b2_prep_regime_sonar | level: B2 | topic: prepositional_regimes | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The verb 'soñar' (to dream about) requires the preposition 'con' in Spanish (soñar con alguien/algo).

### Context
English: "Last night I dreamed about you."

### Exercise
Anoche soñé ___ ti.

<!-- SOLUTION
con
