# Prepositional Regimes: Darse Cuenta
<!-- id: b2_prep_regime_darse_cuenta | level: B2 | topic: prepositional_regimes | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The verbal phrase 'darse cuenta' (to realize) must be followed by the preposition 'de' before its object.

### Context
English: "I realized the mistake."

### Exercise
Me di cuenta ___ el error.

<!-- SOLUTION
de
