# Prepositional Regimes: Depender
<!-- id: b2_prep_regime_depender | level: B2 | topic: prepositional_regimes | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The verb 'depender' (to depend on) requires the preposition 'de' in Spanish.

### Context
English: "It depends on the weather."

### Exercise
Depende ___ el clima.

<!-- SOLUTION
de
