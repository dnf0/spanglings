# Prepositional Regimes 05: 'Insistir en'
<!-- id: b2_prep_insistir_en | level: B2 | topic: prepositional_regimes | type: cloze | concepts: ["prepositional_regimes"] | prerequisites: ["por_vs_para_foundations"] | grammar_focus: "Verbal prepositional regimes (régimen preposicional) with required prepositions." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Insistir' governs the preposition 'en' before nouns, infinitives, or 'que' clauses.

### Context
English: "She insisted on paying the bill for dinner."

### Exercise
Ella insistió ___ pagar la cuenta de la cena.

<!-- SOLUTION
en
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
en pagar
-->
