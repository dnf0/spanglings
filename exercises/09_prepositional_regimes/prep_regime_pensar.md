# Prepositional Regimes: Pensar
<!-- id: b2_prep_regime_pensar | level: B2 | topic: prepositional_regimes | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Pensar en' means to think about / have on one's mind, whereas 'pensar de' means to have an opinion about.

### Context
English: "I am thinking about my vacation."

### Exercise
Estoy pensando ___ mis vacaciones.

<!-- SOLUTION
en
