# Verbal Periphrases 06: Quedar en + Infinitive
<!-- id: c1_periphrasis_quedar_en | level: C1 | topic: verbal_periphrases | type: cloze | concepts: ["advanced_verbal_periphrases"] | prerequisites: ["verbal_periphrases_aspect"] | grammar_focus: "Advanced modal and aspectual periphrases in formal Spanish." -->

> **Grammar & Lexicon**: The periphrasis **quedar en + infinitive** denotes reaching a shared agreement to undertake a mutual task.

### Context
English: "During the sprint retrospective, we agreed to automate regression tests before next week."

### Exercise
Durante la retrospectiva del sprint, (quedamos en) ___ automatizar las pruebas de regresión antes de la próxima semana.

<!-- SOLUTION
quedamos en
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
acordamos
-->
