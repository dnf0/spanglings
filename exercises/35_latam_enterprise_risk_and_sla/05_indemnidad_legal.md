# LatAm Enterprise SLA 05: Indemnification & Hold Harmless
<!-- id: c1_sla_indemnidad_legal | level: C1 | topic: latam_enterprise_risk_and_sla | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar & Lexicon**: The legal obligation to hold a client harmless against third-party intellectual property or security infringement claims is expressed as **mantener indemne a** (or *obligación de indemnidad*).

### Context
English: "The SaaS vendor agrees to hold the enterprise client harmless against third-party intellectual property infringement claims."

### Exercise
El proveedor de software se compromete a (mantener indemne al cliente frente a / mantener indemne al cliente frente a) ___ cualquier reclamación por infracción de propiedad intelectual.

<!-- SOLUTION
mantener indemne al cliente frente a
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
mantener indemne al cliente ante
indemnizar al cliente frente a
