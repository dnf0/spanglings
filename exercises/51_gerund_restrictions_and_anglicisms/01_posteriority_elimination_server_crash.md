# Gerund Restrictions 01: Prohibition of Gerund of Posteriority

> **Grammar Rule**: In Spanish, gerunds cannot express a consequence or action occurring chronologically *after* the main verb (*El servidor falló y provocó...* NOT *\*falló provocando...*).

### Context
