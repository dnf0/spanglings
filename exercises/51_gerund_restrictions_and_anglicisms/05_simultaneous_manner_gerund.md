# Gerund Restrictions 05: Legitimate Simultaneous Manner Gerund

> **Grammar Rule**: Gerunds are fully grammatical when denoting how an action occurs simultaneously with the main verb (*examinó revisando los registros*).

### Context
English: "The auditor examined the architecture by carefully reviewing the audit logs during the inspection."
