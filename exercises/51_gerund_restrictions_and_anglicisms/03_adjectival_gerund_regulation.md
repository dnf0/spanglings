# Gerund Restrictions 03: Prohibition of Adjectival Gerunds
<!-- id: c1_gerund_adjectival_regulation | level: C1 | topic: gerund_restrictions_and_anglicisms | type: cloze | concepts: ["gerund_restrictions_adjectival"] | prerequisites: ["gerund_restrictions_posteriority"] | grammar_focus: "Replacing invalid adjectival gerunds modifying non-fluid nouns with relative clauses." | contrast_note: "Relative clause (*que regula*) vs anglicism calque (*regulando*)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Gerunds cannot function as adjectival modifiers of nouns in Spanish (*una ley que regula* NOT *\*una ley regulando*).

### Context
English: "Parliament passed a directive regulating privacy in artificial intelligence models."

**Why**: Gerunds cannot function as adjectival noun modifiers (*gerundio especificativo*); standard Spanish grammar requires a restrictive relative clause (*que + indicativo*).
