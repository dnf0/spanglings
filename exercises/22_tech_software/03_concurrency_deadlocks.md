# Tech Spanish 03: Concurrency & Deadlocks
<!-- id: c1_tech_concurrency | level: C1 | topic: tech_software | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar & Lexicon**: In systems programming, "deadlock" translates to **bloqueo mutuo** or **interbloqueo**, while "race condition" is **condición de carrera**.

### Context
English: "When two threads attempt to acquire the same resources in reverse order, a deadlock occurs that halts execution."

### Exercise
Cuando dos hilos intentan adquirir los mismos recursos en orden inverso, se produce un (bloqueo mutuo) ___ que detiene la ejecución.

<!-- SOLUTION
bloqueo mutuo
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
interbloqueo
-->
