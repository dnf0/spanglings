# Subjunctive Conjunctions 04: Condition Clauses (Con tal de que)
<!-- id: b1_subj_conj_con_tal_de_que | level: B1 | topic: subjunctive_conjunctions | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Con tal de que' (provided that / as long as) requires the subjunctive mood.

### Context
English: "I will lend you the car provided that you return it on Monday."

### Exercise
Te prestaré el coche con tal de que me lo (devolver) ___ el lunes.

<!-- SOLUTION
devuelvas
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
devuelvas tú
-->
