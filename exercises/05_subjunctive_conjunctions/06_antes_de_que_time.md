# Subjunctive Conjunctions 06: Prior Time (Antes de que)

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Antes de que' (before) always takes the subjunctive, regardless of whether the clause is past, present, or future.

### Context
English: "Let's finish the project before the director arrives."
