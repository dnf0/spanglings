# Subjunctive: Sin que
<!-- id: b2_subj_conj_sin_que | level: B2 | topic: subjunctive_conjunctions | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Sin que' (without) always triggers the subjunctive. If the main verb is in the past, use the imperfect subjunctive.

### Context
English: "She left without anyone seeing her."

### Exercise
Se fue sin que nadie la (ver) ___ .

<!-- SOLUTION
viera
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
viese
-->
