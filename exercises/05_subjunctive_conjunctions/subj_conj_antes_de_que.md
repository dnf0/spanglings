# Subjunctive: Antes de que
<!-- id: b2_subj_conj_antes_de_que | level: B2 | topic: subjunctive_conjunctions | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Antes de que' (before) always triggers the subjunctive, regardless of whether the event is certain or past.

### Context
English: "Call me before they leave."

### Exercise
Llámame antes de que ellos (salir) ___ .

<!-- SOLUTION
salgan
