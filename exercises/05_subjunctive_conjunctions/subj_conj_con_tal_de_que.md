# Subjunctive: Con tal de que
<!-- id: b2_subj_conj_con_tal_de_que | level: B2 | topic: subjunctive_conjunctions | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'Con tal de que' (provided that / as long as) always triggers the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "You can play provided you finish your homework."

### Exercise
Puedes jugar con tal de que (terminar) ___ la tarea.

<!-- SOLUTION
termines
