# Subjunctive: A menos que
<!-- id: b2_subj_conj_a_menos_que | level: B2 | topic: subjunctive_conjunctions | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'A menos que' (unless) always triggers the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "We won't go unless it's hot."

### Exercise
No iremos a menos que (hacer) ___ calor.

<!-- SOLUTION
haga
-->
