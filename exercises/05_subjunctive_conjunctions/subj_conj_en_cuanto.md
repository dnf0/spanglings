# Subjunctive: Conjunctions of Time
<!-- id: b2_subj_conj_en_cuanto | level: B2 | topic: subjunctive_conjunctions | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Conjunctions of time (en cuanto, tan pronto como, cuando) trigger the subjunctive when referring to future, uncompleted events.

### Context
English: "As soon as I arrive, I will call you."

### Exercise
En cuanto (llegar) ___ , te llamaré.

<!-- SOLUTION
llegue
