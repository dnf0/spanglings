# Nuanced Collocations: Harboring Hopes
<!-- id: c1_collocation_albergar | level: C1 | topic: nuanced_collocations | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The verb 'albergar' is the standard high-level collocation used with 'esperanzas' (albergar esperanzas) or 'dudas' to mean harboring hopes or doubts.

### Context
English: "I still harbor hopes of winning the prize."

### Exercise
Todavía yo (harbor) ___ esperanzas de ganar el premio.

<!-- SOLUTION
albergo
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
tengo
-->
