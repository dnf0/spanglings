# Nuanced Collocations: Complying with Law
<!-- id: c1_collocation_acatar | level: C1 | topic: nuanced_collocations | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The verb 'acatar' is a formal verb that collocates with 'ley', 'órdenes', or 'normas' (acatar la ley), meaning to comply with, obey, or respect them.

### Context
English: "Everyone must comply with the law."

### Exercise
Todos deben (comply with) ___ la ley.

<!-- SOLUTION
acatar
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
cumplir
