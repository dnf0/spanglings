# Nuanced Collocations: Taking Effect
<!-- id: c1_collocation_surtir | level: C1 | topic: nuanced_collocations | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The verb 'surtir' collocates with 'efecto' (surtir efecto) to describe medicine, measures, or advice taking effect or producing the desired results.

### Context
English: "The medicine did not take effect."

### Exercise
La medicina no (surtir) ___ efecto.

<!-- SOLUTION
surtió
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
hizo
-->
