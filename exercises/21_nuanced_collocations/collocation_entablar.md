# Nuanced Collocations: Striking up Conversation
<!-- id: c1_collocation_entablar | level: C1 | topic: nuanced_collocations | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The verb 'entablar' forms a high-level, precise collocation with 'conversación' (entablar conversación), meaning to strike up or initiate a conversation.

### Context
English: "I tried to strike up a conversation with him."

### Exercise
Intenté (strike up) ___ conversación con él.

<!-- SOLUTION
entablar
-->
