# Nuanced Collocations: Settling a Matter
<!-- id: c1_collocation_zanjar | level: C1 | topic: nuanced_collocations | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: The verb 'zanjar' collocates elegantly with nouns like 'asunto', 'disputa', or 'debate' (zanjar el asunto), meaning to settle or resolve it decisively and once and for all.

### Context
English: "We must settle the matter once and for all today."

### Exercise
Debemos (settle) ___ el asunto hoy mismo.

<!-- SOLUTION
zanjar
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
resolver
-->
