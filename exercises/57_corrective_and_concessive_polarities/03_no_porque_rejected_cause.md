# Rejected Causal Clauses 03: No Porque (Subjunctive Trigger)
<!-- id: b2_polarity_no_porque_subjunctive | level: B2 | topic: corrective_and_concessive_polarities | type: cloze | concepts: ["rejected_cause_consecutive_de_ahi_que"] | prerequisites: ["corrective_negation_no_es_que_sino_que"] | grammar_focus: "'No porque' rejects an alleged causal motivation and obligatorily governs the SUBJUNCTIVE mood." | contrast_note: "'No porque sea novedoso' (subjunctive rejected cause) vs 'porque es novedoso' (indicative affirmed cause)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: *No porque* introduces a rejected causal explanation and strictly governs the SUBJUNCTIVE mood (*No porque sea fácil significa que debamos hacerlo*).

### Context
English: "Just because a tool is novel does not mean that it should be blindly adopted across the company."

### Exercise
No porque una herramienta (sea / es) ___ novedosa significa que deba adoptarse ciegamente en toda la empresa.

<!-- SOLUTION
sea
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
Sea
