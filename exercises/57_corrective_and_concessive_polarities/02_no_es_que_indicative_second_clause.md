# Corrective Negation 02: Sino Que (Asserted Reality)
<!-- id: b2_polarity_sino_que_indicative | level: B2 | topic: corrective_and_concessive_polarities | type: cloze | concepts: ["corrective_negation_no_es_que_sino_que"] | prerequisites: ["adversative_sino_que_clauses"] | grammar_focus: "In 'No es que [Subjuntivo]... sino que [Indicativo]', the second clause states the actual fact and takes the INDICATIVE." | contrast_note: "'...sino que carece' (indicative assertion) vs invalid *'...sino que carezca' (erroneous subjunctive extension)." -->

> **Grammar Rule**: In contrast to the first clause with *no es que*, the corrective clause introduced by *sino que* asserts factual reality and takes the INDICATIVE mood (*...sino que la dirección carece de presupuesto*).

### Context
English: "It's not that the technical proposal is unviable, but rather executive management lacks budget for this quarter."

### Exercise
No es que la propuesta técnica sea inviable, sino que la dirección ejecutiva (carece / carezca) ___ de presupuesto para este trimestre.

<!-- SOLUTION
carece
-->

Carece
