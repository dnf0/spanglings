# Formal Consecutive Link 05: De Ahí Que (Subjunctive Formula)

> **Grammar Rule**: *De ahí que* introduces a formal consequence and obligatorily governs the SUBJUNCTIVE mood (*De ahí que decidiéramos actuar*).
