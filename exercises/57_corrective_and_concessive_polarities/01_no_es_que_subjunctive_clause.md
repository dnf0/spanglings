> **Grammar Rule**: *No es que* rejects a false assumption or hypothesis and obligatorily governs the SUBJUNCTIVE mood (*No es que no queramos colaborar*).

### Context
English: "It's not that we don't want to collaborate on the API integration, but rather our schedule is saturated."
