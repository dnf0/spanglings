# Negated Perception: No veo que
<!-- id: c1_negated_perception_veo | level: C1 | topic: negated_perception | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: Negated verbs of physical or mental perception ('no ver que', 'no notar que') trigger the subjunctive when they imply doubt or rejection.

### Context
English: "I don't see that it has any advantage."

### Exercise
No veo que (tener) ___ ninguna ventaja.

<!-- SOLUTION
tenga
-->
