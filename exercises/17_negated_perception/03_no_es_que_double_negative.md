# Negated Perception 03: 'No es que no' Double Clause
<!-- id: c1_neg_perception_no_es_que | level: C1 | topic: negated_perception | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'No es que...' (It's not that...) always takes the subjunctive mood to reject a presupposed cause.

### Context
English: "It's not that I don't want to go, it's that I don't have time."

### Exercise
No es que no (querer) ___ ir, es que no tengo tiempo libre.

<!-- SOLUTION
quiera
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
quiera yo
-->
