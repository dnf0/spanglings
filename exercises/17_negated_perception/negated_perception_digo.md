# Negated Perception: No digo que
<!-- id: c1_negated_perception_digo | level: C1 | topic: negated_perception | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: While affirmative declaration verbs (decir, asegurar) take the indicative, their negated forms ('no digo que', 'no aseguro que') trigger the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "I'm not saying it is easy."

### Exercise
No digo que (ser) ___ fácil.

<!-- SOLUTION
sea
