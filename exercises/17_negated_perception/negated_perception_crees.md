# Negated Perception: Question No crees que
<!-- id: c1_negated_perception_crees | level: C1 | topic: negated_perception | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: When a negated verb of belief ('no creer', 'no pensar') is formulated as a question, it seeks agreement on a shared opinion and takes the indicative.

### Context
English: "Don't you think she is intelligent?"

### Exercise
¿No crees que ella (ser) ___ inteligente?

<!-- SOLUTION
es
