# Negated Perception 05: 'No parece que'
<!-- id: c1_neg_perception_no_parece | level: C1 | topic: negated_perception | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'No parece que' (It does not seem that) triggers subjunctive, in contrast to affirmative 'parece que' which takes indicative.

### Context
English: "It does not seem that the situation will improve soon."

### Exercise
No parece que la situación (ir) ___ a mejorar pronto.

<!-- SOLUTION
vaya
-->

<!-- ALTERNATIVES
vaya a mejorar
-->
