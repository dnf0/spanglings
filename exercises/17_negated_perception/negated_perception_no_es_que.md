# Negated Perception: No es que
<!-- id: c1_negated_perception_no_es_que | level: C1 | topic: negated_perception | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: 'No es que' (it's not that) always triggers the subjunctive. This holds true even with double negatives ('no es que no + subjunctive').

### Context
English: "It's not that I don't want to go."

### Exercise
No es que no (querer) ___ ir.

<!-- SOLUTION
quiera
