# Negated Perception: No me parece que
<!-- id: c1_negated_perception_parece | level: C1 | topic: negated_perception | type: cloze -->

> **Grammar Rule**: While 'me parece que' (it seems to me that) takes the indicative, its negated counterpart 'no me parece que' triggers the subjunctive.

### Context
English: "It doesn't seem to me that it is correct."

### Exercise
No me parece que (ser) ___ correcto.

<!-- SOLUTION
sea
